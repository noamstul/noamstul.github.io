About Me

I specialize in front-end web development, utilizing HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and modern frameworks like Vue to build responsive web applications with a focus on user experience design and digital accessibility -- I believe strongly in the web being accessible for all.

I have over 5 years' experience with web development, and I am proficient with many different web development tools, from APIs to content and learning management systems.

Though my day job has me building accessible and engaging e-learning components for the Federal Public Service, I am also a freelance web developer.

Are you interested in my work? Feel free to contact me to learn more about what I do.
